# Expert check, concept C v2 (gate 4B), 30 min

Goal: see if C v2 helps an analyst decide faster and trust the result.
We watch how they work, not what they like. Fake data, learning prototype.
Review rules and labels are draft v0, AI-assisted. Do not present them
as validated.

## 0-3 min. Intro
- "These are early sketches, not a product. Nothing you say is wrong.
  Please think aloud."
- Ask permission to take notes.

## 3-10 min. Their work
1. Which tools do you use during triage today, and how many screens?
2. What do you check first before you trust an automated verdict?
3. Does seeing similar past alerts help you decide faster?
4. What made you stop using a tool in the past?

## 10-18 min. Screen 1: list with Quick confirm / Needs review
Task: "Your shift just started. Which alert do you handle first? Why?"
Then:
- "What do you think Quick confirm means here?"
- "Would you confirm these without opening them? When not?"
- "What tells you an alert needs your review?"

## 18-26 min. Screen 2: alert-003 detail (unclear alert)
Task: "This alert is unclear. Show me what you check before you decide."
Then:
- "What would you need to see to trust this verdict?"
- Point at the tick "I checked the evidence": "Would you tick this
  every time, or on autopilot?"
- "If the AI is wrong here, what do you do next?"

## 26-29 min. Close
- "What is missing for a busy shift?"
- "What in your current tool must not get lost?"

## 29-30 min
- "Can I send you one follow-up question?"
